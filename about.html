About Subcon

Experience you can build on.

Subcon is a residential concrete company built around practical industry knowledge, a large skilled team and consistent delivery across Melbourne and Ballarat.

Our story

Built from hands-on experience.

Founded by Ray Spiteri

Subcon was founded by Ray Spiteri, whose concrete-industry experience spans more than 20 years. Over more than 12 years, Subcon has grown into a trusted residential concrete business servicing Western Melbourne, Northern Melbourne and the Ballarat region.

A team of more than 50

Subcon's team includes professional concreters, certified excavators and skilled bobcat operators. The company brings that combined experience to residential slab construction, excavation, piers, base preparation, reinforcement and finishing.

Residential concrete focus

The business specialises in residential slab construction and works alongside leading builders. The focus is straightforward: quality workmanship, precision, safety and reliable project delivery.

Commitment to the result

Subcon's existing site has always centred on workmanship, customer service and complete project satisfaction. The same priorities guide the business today—professional delivery and a slab built to meet the requirements of the home above it.
