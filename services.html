Services

The slab process, covered.

Subcon P/L delivers the key stages of residential slab construction across Western Melbourne, Northern Melbourne and the Ballarat region.

Core services

From excavation
to final finish.

The services below are drawn directly from Subcon's existing company material and reflect the work the team performs across residential slab construction.

01

Piers Pouring

Reliable pier construction for stable residential foundations.

Piers form part of the foundation system where required. Subcon's experienced team handles the preparation and concrete work needed to support the slab and the structure above it.

02

Base Preparation

Ground and base preparation before the slab stage.

Accurate preparation is essential before reinforcement and pouring begin. Subcon provides the excavation, base work and site preparation required to move the project into the slab stage with a solid foundation.

03

Steel Reinforcement

Reinforcement installation for structural integrity.

Subcon prepares and installs slab reinforcement in line with the project requirements, creating the reinforced structure needed before concrete placement.

04

Slab Pouring & Finishing

Concrete placement and finish for residential slabs.

The team's core specialty is residential slab construction, with attention to concrete placement, levels, finish quality, durability and the requirements of the build.

05

Excavation & Site Operations

Certified excavators and skilled bobcat operators.

Subcon's existing company profile identifies certified excavators and bobcat operators within its team, supporting the excavation and site-preparation stages that feed into the slab process.

06

Project Consulting

Practical input backed by long-term concrete experience.

With Ray Spiteri's more than 20 years in the concrete industry and the team's residential slab experience, Subcon provides practical guidance and recommendations around concrete project requirements.

The finish matters

Precision at every stage.

Subcon's existing company site highlights superior finishes, durable slabs and timely project completion as core features of its work. Those priorities run through the process from preparation to final concrete finishing.
